II · THE JOURNEY

Day by day

Ten days, gently paced. Private transfers throughout; mornings earned, evenings savoured.

Day 1 · Fri 18 Sep

Touch down in Saigon

Land at Tan Son Nhat at 16:15. Grab a Viettel e-SIM at the airport, clear visas, and be at the villa by about 6pm. Then an easy first evening — dinner and a rooftop bar over the city. No alarms.

Arrive 16:15Viettel e-SIMRooftop Bar
Day 2 · Sat 19 Sep

Saigon — tunnels & Bui Vien

The Cu Chi Tunnels on a private tour with the shooting-range experience — book the Klook tour that includes pickup from the villa. Back to the city for the evening, then out on Bui Vien party street.

Cu Chi TunnelsShooting RangeBui Vien
Day 3 · Sun 20 Sep

Saigon — gloves on, forks out

A Muay Thai / kickboxing class to sweat out the night before, then eat properly: Hum Garden (Michelin-recognised vegetarian), Anan Saigon (Michelin star) — and Pizza 4P's is the must-try wildcard.

Muay Thai ClassAnan SaigonPizza 4P's
Day 4 · Mon 21 Sep

Saigon → Hanoi → night train

VN 246 leaves Tan Son Nhat's Terminal 3 at 11am and lands in Hanoi at 1:10pm; hotel by 3–3:30pm, then tailoring in the afternoon. At 10pm, board the overnight train to Sapa — beds on wheels, breakfast in the mountains.

VN 246 · 11:00 → 13:10TailoringNight Train 22:00
Day 5 · Tue 22 Sep

Into the hills — Sapa

Arrive at 6am and transfer up to Lady Hill Sapa — the hotel books a 10-seater minivan for about A$56. Then the rice terraces and the Glass Bridge for the brave.

Arrive 06:00Lady Hill SapaGlass Bridge
Day 6 · Wed 23 Sep

Sapa — full day, then roll out

A full day in the mountains — trek the valleys, chase views, take it slow. At 10pm, the overnight train back down to Hanoi.

Valley TrekNight Train 22:00
Day 7 · Thu 24 Sep

Train → Halong Bay cruise

Roll into Hanoi at 6am and go straight on — a pre-booked transfer to the coast to board the overnight cruise by 11am. Kayaks, karsts, sundeck sunset, seafood dinner.

Arrive 06:00Board 11:00Overnight Cruise
Day 8 · Fri 25 Sep

Bay → Hanoi

The cruise docks at 10am; Hanoi hotel from 2pm. Then the city: shopping and sussing out the vibes, Train Street, egg coffee at Café Giảng where it was invented, and a Muay Thai class for the keen. Food list: Mr Bảy Miền Tây (Michelin-Guide bánh xèo), Hanoi Corner Food, Highlands Coffee, Fuku Coffee & Matcha.

Train StreetEgg CoffeeMr Bảy Miền Tây
Day 9 · Sat 26 Sep

Caves & karsts — Ninh Binh

Day trip south to Ninh Binh — Hoa Lu ancient capital, rowing boats through Trang An, and the Mua Cave viewpoint climb. Book the Klook day tour from Hanoi — there's a limo luxury option if the group's feeling fancy.

Hoa LuTrang An BoatsMua Cave
Day 10 · Sun 27 Sep

Tạm biệt, Vietnam

A last Hanoi breakfast, then to Noi Bai for the 16:00 flight home. Bags full of tailored suits, terrace photos, and a group chat that won't shut up for months.

IV · GOOD TO KNOW

The know-how

The small print that keeps nine people moving smoothly.

i

Visas

Most nationalities need a Vietnam e-visa — apply online 2–4 weeks out, all nine together.

ii

Internal Travel

All locked in: VN 246 SGN→HAN, 11:00 on Sep 21 (lands 13:10, departs Terminal 3), plus sleeper cabins on the Sapa night trains (Sep 21 & 23).

iii

Money

VND cash for markets & street food; cards at hotels and restaurants. A Wise / travel card works well — pull cash from ATMs on arrival and carry small notes. (Yes, Gautam, this one's for you.)

iv

Weather

~28–33°C and humid in the cities; Sapa runs much cooler in the hills — pack a layer. Short showers likely everywhere.

v

Getting Around

Grab is Vietnam's Uber/Gojek — cars and bikes, everywhere. Pre-arranged vans for the group on transfer days; the Sapa hotel books the minivan up the hill.

vi

Connectivity

Grab a Viettel e-SIM at Tan Son Nhat when you land — best coverage, including up in Sapa.

$2.5–3.8kper person · ground costs
