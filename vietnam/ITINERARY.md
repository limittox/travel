# Vietnam — The Nine · 18–27 September 2026

**9 travellers · 9 nights · south-to-north**

Route: **Saigon → Hanoi → Sapa → Halong Bay → Ninh Binh → Hanoi**

Flights are open-jaw — you land in **Ho Chi Minh City** and fly home from **Hanoi** — so the trip runs south to north. One internal flight (Saigon→Hanoi) and two overnight sleeper trains (Hanoi⇄Sapa) tie it together. September is shoulder season — warm and humid in the cities, cool in the Sapa hills; pack a layer and a compact rain shell.

## Day by day

### Day 1 · Fri 18 Sep — Arrive Saigon
Land at Tan Son Nhat at 16:15. **Viettel e-SIM** at the airport, visas, hotel by ~18:00. Dinner and a rooftop bar.

### Day 2 · Sat 19 Sep — Cu Chi Tunnels & Bui Vien
**Cu Chi Tunnels private tour with shooting-range experience** — book the [Klook tour with hotel pickup](https://www.klook.com/en-AU/activity/856-cu-chi-tunnels-private-tour-ho-chi-minh/). Evening: **Bui Vien** party street.

### Day 3 · Sun 20 Sep — Saigon (Airbnb)
**Muay Thai / kickboxing class**, then the food hit-list: **Hum Garden** (Michelin-recognised), **Anan Saigon** (Michelin star), and the must-try **Pizza 4P's**.

### Day 4 · Mon 21 Sep — Saigon → Hanoi → night train
11:00 flight to Hanoi, arrive 13:30. Hotel by 15:00–15:30, **tailoring** in the afternoon. **22:00 overnight train to Sapa.**

### Day 5 · Tue 22 Sep — Sapa
Arrive 06:00. Transfer to **Lady Hill Sapa** — the hotel books a 10-seater minivan (~A$56). Rice terraces and the **Glass Bridge**.

### Day 6 · Wed 23 Sep — Sapa → night train
Full day in the mountains. **22:00 overnight train back to Hanoi.**

### Day 7 · Thu 24 Sep — Halong Bay cruise
Arrive Hanoi 06:00, pre-booked transfer to the coast, **board the overnight cruise by 11:00**.

### Day 8 · Fri 25 Sep — Bay → Hanoi
Cruise docks 10:00; Hanoi hotel from 14:00. Shopping and sussing out the vibes, **Train Street**, egg coffee at **Café Giảng** (where it was invented), optional Muay Thai class. Food: **Mr Bảy Miền Tây** (Michelin-Guide bánh xèo), Hanoi Corner Food, Highlands Coffee, Fuku Coffee & Matcha.

### Day 9 · Sat 26 Sep — Ninh Binh day trip
Hoa Lu, Trang An boats, Mua Cave — [Klook day tour from Hanoi](https://www.klook.com/en-AU/activity/221960-ninh-binh-day-tour-from-hanoi-hoa-lu-trang-an-mua-cave/), with a limo luxury option.

### Day 10 · Sun 27 Sep — Depart
Breakfast, then to Noi Bai for the **16:00 flight home**. Tạm biệt, Vietnam.

## Practical notes

- **Visa:** Most nationalities need a Vietnam e-visa — apply online ~2–4 weeks ahead, all 9 together.
- **Money:** VND cash for markets & street food; cards fine at hotels/restaurants. A Wise/travel card works well; pull cash from ATMs on arrival and carry small notes.
- **Apps:** **Grab** is Vietnam's Uber/Gojek equivalent — cars and bikes, works everywhere.
- **Weather (Sep):** ~28–33°C and humid in the cities; Sapa is much cooler — pack a layer. Short showers likely.
- **Connectivity:** Viettel e-SIM from the airport — best coverage, including Sapa.
- **Indicative ground budget:** roughly **US$2,500–3,800 per person** — refine once the remaining rooms are locked.
