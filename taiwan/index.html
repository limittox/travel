Twelve days, day by day

週六SAT19十二月Dec
台北Taipei

Land, Soak, Go Out

Land at 04:20 and take the Airport MRT into Taipei Main (about 40 minutes), then go to Meander in Ximending. Book the room from the night of the 18th so you can sleep from 6am instead of waiting for a 3pm check-in. Sleep until lunch.

Afternoon: a private hot-spring room in Beitou (about NT$1,000–1,500 for two, 60–120 min) to recover from the overnight flight. Evening: Ximending street food, meet people in the hostel common room, then Saturday night in Xinyi at WAVE or AI Club.

Option B: you land in the evening, so skip Beitou and go straight out.

週日SUN20十二月Dec
十分 · 九份Shifen · Jiufen

Lanterns & Jiufen

Late start. Train to Ruifang, then the little Pingxi Line to Shifen. Write your wishes on a sky lantern (NT$200–350, cash) and launch it from the old railway track, then walk to Shifen Waterfall. Taxi up to Jiufen (about NT$200–250 from Ruifang) for when the red lanterns come on at dusk. It's packed on a Sunday, so arrive after 4pm.

Train back via Ruifang to Songshan and walk straight into Raohe Night Market, next to the station.

週一MON21十二月Dec
小琉球Xiaoliuqiu

Swim With Turtles

High-speed rail from Taipei to Zuoying (Kaohsiung), about 1.5–2 hours. Pick up a one-way rental car at Zuoying, to drop off in Hualien on the 24th. Drive about an hour to Donggang, park at the ferry terminal and take the 20–25 minute boat to Xiaoliuqiu (about NT$200–230 each way).

Rent electric scooters (one rider each, no licence needed) and lap the coral island. Snorkel with green turtles straight off the shore. The water is 22–26°C, so hire a wetsuit. Turtles are likely but not guaranteed. Then Vase Rock, and sunset on the west side. It's a quiet island night: 7-Eleven beers on the harbour wall.

週二TUE22十二月Dec
南迴 → 鹿野 → 都蘭Southlink → Luye → Dulan

Fly Over the Valley

Early ferry back to the car, then drive the Southlink Highway over the bottom of Taiwan's mountains to the Pacific (about 3 hours). Afternoon: tandem paragliding off Luye Highland over the rice fields of the East Rift Valley. It's NT$3,300, paid in cash, with about 10–15 minutes in the air. December is outside the calmest season, so book with a weather backup in mind. Plan B is a soak at Zhiben hot springs.

Evening: up the coast to Dulan and check into WaGaLiGong. It's a surf hostel with its own bar and pizzeria, and the easiest place on the east coast to meet people.

週三WED23十二月Dec
都蘭 → 花蓮Dulan → Hualien

Surf, Then Highway 11

A morning surf lesson in Dulan. The northeast monsoon makes winter the best surf season on this coast. It can also get big, so let the coach pick a beginner-friendly spot. Email WaGaLiGong ahead to make sure a coach will be there.

Afternoon: Highway 11 north along the Pacific, Taiwan's best coastal drive. Stop at Sanxiantai's eight-arch footbridge and the rock terraces at Shitiping, then on to Hualien (about 3 hours plus stops). Night: Dongdamen Night Market.

週四THU24十二月Dec
太魯閣 → 台北Taroko → Taipei

Marble Canyon, Christmas Eve

Early start into Taroko Gorge: the Qingshui Cliffs lookout, Taroko Terrace, then Highway 8 up to Tianxiang (open in the daytime only) for the short Tabido trail. Some of the famous parts (Swallow Grotto, Shakadang and the Tunnel of Nine Turns) are still closed after the 2024 earthquake. Having your own car is the easy way in, because there's still no bus up the gorge.

Drop the car in Hualien by about 1pm and take the Taroko or Puyuma Express to Taipei (about 2h15). These sell out on Christmas Eve. Tickets go on sale at midnight 28 days ahead, which for this train means 26 November.

Christmas Eve: check into the Xinyi hotel, see the Christmasland lights in Banqiao if they're still up (2026 dates aren't announced; it usually runs mid-November to late December), then cocktails at To Infinity & Beyond, #35 on Asia's 50 Best Bars.

週五FRI25十二月Dec
台北 · 聖誕節Taipei · Christmas Day

Christmas at 460 Metres

The special one. Morning: Skyline 460, a harnessed walk around the outside of Taipei 101, 460 metres up. It's NT$3,000 each, about 50 minutes, in groups of three. It's cancelled in bad weather and sells out, so book the moment Christmas Day slots open. Wear trousers and flat shoes.

Late afternoon: the stairs up Elephant Mountain for sunset over Taipei 101 (the photo at the top of this page). Then Christmas dinner on the 48th floor at CÉ LA VI, and on to the clubs. Christmas Day is now a public holiday in Taiwan and falls on a Friday this year, so Xinyi will be packed.

萬里 · 台北Wanli · Taipei

Paraglide, Then the Big One

Day: tandem paragliding at Wanli on the north coast, under an hour from Taipei, flying over Green Bay. Walk-in flights have been about NT$2,000, or about US$170 booked online with transfers and video. Winter winds cancel some days. The backup is the Maokong Gondola up to the tea houses.

Night: the biggest one. A crawl along Lane 160 of Dunhua South Road: Bar Mood (#82 in Asia, with cocktails built on Taiwanese landscapes), To Infinity & Beyond on the same lane if you skipped it, then The Public House (#92) and a club to finish.

週日SUN27十二月Dec
日月潭Sun Moon Lake

Lake Days

Slow morning, then the high-speed train to Taichung (about 1 hour) and the bus to Sun Moon Lake (about 1.5 hours). Take the ropeway over the hills to the Formosan Aboriginal Culture Village. Your ticket covers the ropeway and the theme-park rides. Sunset on the water.

Your Christmas treat: a night at The Lalu with its famous lake-edge infinity pool. Or get a lake-view room in Shuishe for a fraction of the price. It's the last day of the long weekend, so expect crowds.

須知
read before you go

The intel

一Should we drive?開車

Yes, but only on the east coast (21–24 Dec, Kaohsiung → Taitung → Hualien). The Southlink and Highway 11 are the best drives in Taiwan, buses there are infrequent, and Taroko still has no bus up the gorge. Everywhere else the trains are faster than a car, and nobody wants to drive in Taipei. Get an International Driving Permit (car class) before you leave and bring your Australian licence too. It's valid for 30 days after you land. Scooters need a motorcycle licence, so skip them unless you have one. Electric scooters on Xiaoliuqiu don't need a licence. No turning right on a red light.

二Entry入境

Australians get 90 days visa-free, and your passport must be valid for 6 months. Fill in the free Taiwan Arrival Card (TWAC) online at twac.immigration.gov.tw in the 3 days before you land. Ignore copycat sites that charge a fee.

三Weather天氣

Taipei: 16–21°C, often drizzly (around 13 wet days in December). The south and east: 18–25°C and mostly dry, with the sea at about 24–25°C. Pack layers and a light rain jacket. That's why the outdoor activities are in the south and east.

四Trains火車

The high-speed rail runs down the west side, and Taiwan Railway covers the east. Taiwan Railway tickets go on sale at midnight 28 days ahead, and the Hualien → Taipei train on the 24th will sell out. Set a reminder for 26 Nov. You can't tap an EasyCard onto the reserved express trains.

五Christmas聖誕

25 December is now a public holiday in Taiwan. This year it's a Friday, so 25–27 is a long weekend. Hotels, trains and Sun Moon Lake get busier and dearer. Book those first.

六Taroko太魯閣

Partly open. Qingshui Cliffs, Taroko Terrace and Tianxiang (Highway 8, daytime only) are open. Swallow Grotto, Shakadang and the Tunnel of Nine Turns are closed. Check the national park site the week before.

七Money錢

About NT$22–23 to A$1. Card works in hotels and bars, but bring cash for night markets, lanterns, paragliding (cash on the day) and ferries. Get an EasyCard for the MRT, buses and 7-Eleven.

八Backup plans備案

Both paraglides, Skyline 460 and the surf lesson all depend on the weather. Book with free cancellation where you can, and keep the backups in the plan: Zhiben hot springs, Maokong and Beitou.
